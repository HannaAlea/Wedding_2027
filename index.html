Chapter One

The Beginning

After months of chatting and watching movies and series together online, we finally decided to meet in person and watch the new Batman movie at the cinema. It was still during the COVID days, hence the masks!

Chapter Two

First Date

Since we both love amusement parks, we decided to have our first date at TusenFryd. We tried to go on pretty much every ride we could, so by the end of the day, we were completely exhausted! But we had such a great time together.

Chapter Three

Growing Together

Four years have passed since we started this journey together. Since then, we’ve traveled to new places, gone on hikes, joined running races, played pc games, attended concerts, and shared countless ordinary days in between. Through every trip, milestone, adventure, and quiet moment, we’ve learned just how good life can be when it’s shared with someone you love <3

04

The Proposal

Chasing Thrills Together

From traveling across Europe and Asia to attending concerts and exploring amusement parks, we’ve shared plenty of adventures together. Wherever we go, we always make it our mission to find the scariest ride we can. Little did we know, the scariest one was still to come.

3 photos
05

The Next Chapter

She said YES!

Little did Hanna know, this “scariest ride” had actually been planned long before they even met. Back in 2014, when Nathaniel first visited Disneyland Paris with his parents, he knew right then and there that one day, he wanted to propose to his future wife at the happiest place on Earth.


Years later, Hanna thought they were simply visiting another amusement park, just like they always do when traveling somewhere new. What she didn’t know was that Nathaniel had been carrying this plan with him all along.


And this time, the scariest ride wasn’t a roller coaster. It was Nathaniel getting down on one knee and asking the biggest question of his life.

FAQ

A few helpful answers.

Can I bring a plus one?

Please refer to your invitation, where the names of all invited guests are listed.If plus one is included, this will also be clearly stated on your invitation.

Are children invited?

Please refer to the "Kindly Note" section on your invitation. We kindly ask all guests to follow the instructions there.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

You can let us know on the RSVP form and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Is transportation provided for the wedding day?

Transportation will not be provided, so we recommend arranging your own transportation in advance.If you're unsure about your options or need a little help figuring out how to get to the venue or home after the celebration, please reach out to us ahead of time and we'll be happy to find suitable solutions!

Does the venue and church have parking?

Yes, both the venue and church have designated parking areas available for guests.

Can I change my RSVP after the deadline?

Things happen and we understand that circumstances can change. Just reach out to us as soon as you can so we can plan accordingly.

Can I post photos on Social Media?

Definitely! We'd love to see your photos from our special day. Feel free to tag us on social media, and we also encourage you to upload your photos using the QR code that will be provided at the event so we can keep all the wonderful memories in one place.Thank you!
